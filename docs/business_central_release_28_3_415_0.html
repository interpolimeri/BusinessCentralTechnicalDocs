Business Central Release Notes

Monthly Update for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central

Release 28.3.415.0 05/08/2026 Monthly Update

This release introduces new business functionalities, process improvements, usability enhancements and corrections across several areas of Business Central.

The notes below are written for end users and key users, with a focus on what changes in daily activities and which business areas are impacted.

✨ New Features

EU HS Codes and TARIC Management

New tools have been introduced to manage and consult European HS Codes and TARIC classifications directly within Business Central.

These new capabilities simplify the maintenance of customs information and provide easier access to classification data required for international trade activities.

Brand and Product Family Management

New master data structures have been introduced to manage Brands and Manufacturer Families.

This improves product classification, reporting capabilities, and future data analysis activities.

Intercompany Synchronization Enhancements

New synchronization capabilities have been added to support the exchange of information between group companies.

The objective is to reduce manual maintenance activities and improve data consistency across subsidiaries.

Self-Invoice Report

A new Self-Invoice report has been introduced to support local fiscal and compliance requirements.

🚀 Improvements

Improved Delivery Note (DDT) Management

The warehouse shipment process has been enhanced to simplify the issuance and printing of Delivery Notes (DDTs).

A new Shipping Information page allows warehouse operators to manage all shipment-related information before posting, including:

  • Carrier details
  • Shipment method
  • Weights
  • Shipping notes
  • Number of DDT copies to print
Previously, operators often had to complete or adjust information after warehouse posting, and the number of printed copies could not be managed directly as part of the shipment process. With this update, the requested number of copies can be defined from the Shipping Information page and applied when the DDT is generated.

With the new approach, all required shipping information can be entered in a single place before posting. This makes the shipment process faster, reduces administrative effort, and improves the quality and consistency of shipping documents.

DocuFlow and Document Management

Several improvements have been implemented to streamline document management processes.

  • Better handling of duplicate documents
  • Improved validation controls
  • More efficient document processing
  • New analysis tools based on document metadata
  • Performance optimizations

These changes improve both data quality and operational efficiency.

Dynamics 365 CRM Integration

The integration with Dynamics 365 CRM has been enhanced to improve synchronization reliability and ensure better alignment between Business Central and CRM data.

Additional customer information is now available and synchronization processes have been strengthened.

Warehouse and Transfer Operations

Transfer shipment and transfer receipt processes have been enhanced with additional information and operational improvements.

These changes improve document traceability and support more efficient warehouse operations.

Purchasing

The purchase price import process has been improved with additional validations and clearer error messages.

This helps users identify issues more quickly and reduces the risk of importing incorrect purchasing data.

Cash Flow Forecasting

The calculation of expected payment and receipt dates for purchase and sales orders has been refined to improve cash flow forecasting accuracy.

User Experience and Productivity

Several usability improvements have been introduced across the application, including:

  • Simplified user interactions
  • Improved visibility of operational information
  • Better support for daily activities
  • Enhanced navigation and efficiency in selected areas
  • Improved user experience in messaging and operational screens

🪲 Corrections and Stability Improvements

This release also includes a number of corrections and technical improvements aimed at increasing system reliability and compatibility, including:

  • Improvements to order processing workflows
  • Corrections in warehouse and transfer document management
  • Updates to security roles and permissions
  • Improved handling of purchasing and pricing processes
  • Compatibility updates for Business Central version 28
  • Corrections related to transfer documents and role center actions
  • General stability and performance improvements across multiple areas
